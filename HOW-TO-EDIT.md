# Managing Sana's closet

**Site:** https://sanastylebeauty.pages.dev/
**Admin:** https://sanastylebeauty.pages.dev/admin/

Changes go live about 1–2 minutes after you press **Save**.

## Signing in (first time on a device)

1. Open the admin page and choose **Sign In Using Access Token**.
2. Follow the link in the dialog to GitHub. The right permissions are already ticked. Create the token and copy it.
3. Paste it into the dialog. The browser remembers it, so you only do this once per device.

## Add a new product

**Products → New Product**, then fill in:

- **Name:** short, like "Ruffle peplum top" (not the long Amazon title)
- **Brand:** optional
- **Amazon link:** in the Amazon app or SiteStripe, choose **Share → Copy link**
- **Price band** and **Category**
- **Photo:** optional; use your own photo or a crop of your pin

Then press **Save**.

## Add a new look (outfit)

**Looks → New Look**, then:

1. Add the title and occasion.
2. Upload the photos. The first photo is the cover.
3. Under **Pieces in this look**, pick from your products. Type to search.
4. Press **Save**. New looks appear at the top of the home page.

## Everyday changes

| What happened | What to do |
|---|---|
| Item out of stock | Open the product and turn on **Hide from site** |
| Found a better dupe | Open the product and paste the new Amazon link |
| Price changed | Change the **Price band** |
| Move a look to the top | Turn on **Pin to the top**, or change its date |
| Put something in an edit | Add the edit's tag (e.g. `halloween`) to the product or look |

The **Under $30** edit fills itself from price bands.

## Rules from Amazon

- Don't upload Amazon's product photos. Use your own photos or your pin images.
- Don't type exact prices. Use the price bands.
- Always paste your own affiliate link.
